# ResearchRadar Domain Language

ResearchRadar separates source truth, personal research fit, career timing, and user actions so
that one kind of evidence is never mistaken for another.

## Research fit

**Core Domain Fit**:
The opportunity directly addresses cognition, brain, or behavior through computational research.
_Avoid_: High priority, verified opportunity

**Adjacent Domain Fit**:
The opportunity is closely related to the core through neuroscience, neurotechnology, behavioral
science, or cognition-linked AI research, without directly establishing a core research question.
_Avoid_: Core fit, generic relevance

**Technical Transferability**:
The opportunity develops useful methods or research skills but lacks evidence of a cognition,
brain, or behavior research question.
_Avoid_: Domain fit, eligibility

**Low Domain Fit**:
The available evidence identifies work outside the user's research trajectory, such as unrelated
commercial, legal, recruiting, sales, or technical-specialist work.
_Avoid_: Invalid source, excluded opportunity

**Insufficient Evidence**:
The available source evidence cannot support a reliable domain-fit determination.
_Avoid_: Low fit, unknown eligibility

**Research Relevance**:
The deterministic combination of research-domain fit, methodological fit, and research-question
fit. It is separate from PhD career value, technical career fit, compensation, and feedback.
_Avoid_: Overall priority, recommendation

## Recommendation and evidence

**Recommendation Priority**:
A personal review classification derived from research fit and career preferences. It is not a
source fact and cannot change official opportunity history.
_Avoid_: Opportunity status, source verification

**Source Verification**:
Evidence that a monitoring source or posting URL is official. It does not verify every extracted
field or establish personal relevance.
_Avoid_: Adapter health, confirmed opening

**Career Stage**:
A planning label describing when an opportunity may be useful, including future PhD-level career
targets; it is not an eligibility decision.
_Avoid_: Recommendation priority, qualification status

**Career Stage Fit**:
An evidence-based timing classification for a job: near-term internship/RA, pre-PhD research
engineering, post-PhD career, transferable technical work, informational team, or unknown. It is
independent of the job's role function and domain fit.
_Avoid_: Confirmed eligibility, recommendation score

**Application Readiness**:
A conservative statement about whether the currently evidenced requirements support near-term
consideration, require review, describe a future target, or are informational only.
_Avoid_: Admission likelihood, work authorization, assumed qualification

**Listing Status**:
Whether a role is on the current official index, has only an accessible detail page, was not
observed, is explicitly closed, or could not be checked. Missing or failed retrieval is not closure.
_Avoid_: Source health, recommendation priority

**Research Team Profile**:
Verified information about an organization's research activity without evidence of a current job.
_Avoid_: Confirmed opening, talent-pool vacancy

**User Feedback**:
The user's explicit preference, saved state, hidden state, and notes. It remains authoritative and
separate from deterministic scoring.
_Avoid_: Recommendation priority, source evidence
