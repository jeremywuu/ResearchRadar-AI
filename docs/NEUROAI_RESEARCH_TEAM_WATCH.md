# NeuroAI Research Team Watch

This watch is informational. A paper, lab page, grant, or careers landing page does not establish
a current vacancy.

## Meta AI

The official TRIBE v2 publication describes a multimodal foundation model for in-silico
neuroscience across vision, audition, and language. It is strong Track A evidence for brain-model
alignment and neural representations. Meta AI's official careers entry point is separate. Until a
matching role appears on that current careers index, the correct entity is
`RESEARCH_TEAM_PROFILE`, not `CONFIRMED_JOB_OPENING`.

## Google DeepMind

The official hybrid neural-cognitive models publication concerns human reward learning and is
strong cross-track evidence for computational decision science. DeepMind's careers page is a
separate source. The publication alone says nothing about current hiring, eligibility, or posting
status.

Future monitoring should compare current official careers indexes with a small set of verified
team topics. A publication can update a team's research direction; it cannot create a job. A team
without a matching opening remains `INFORMATIONAL_RESEARCH_TEAM`.
