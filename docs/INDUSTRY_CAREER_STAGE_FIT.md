# Industry Career Stage Fit

Career Stage Fit answers *when and how a verified role may be useful*. It does not replace Domain
Fit, Role Function, the numerical score, or an eligibility decision.

| Classification | Meaning | Default readiness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NEAR_TERM_INTERNSHIP_OR_RA` | Official evidence identifies an internship or RA path | Requirements need review |
| `PRE_PHD_RESEARCH_ENGINEERING` | Research or research-adjacent engineering may build pre-PhD experience | Requirements need review |
| `POST_PHD_RESEARCH_CAREER` | PhD or senior research evidence makes it a future pathway | Future career target |
| `TRANSFERABLE_TECHNICAL` | Technical platform work has indirect skill value | Requirements need review |
| `INFORMATIONAL_RESEARCH_TEAM` | Research activity is verified but no opening is verified | Informational only |
| `UNKNOWN` | Evidence is insufficient | Unknown |

The assessment preserves four independent questions: long-term career relevance, current
application readiness, confirmed eligibility, and work-authorization evidence. Academic research
experience is never silently rewritten as professional employment. Missing sponsorship or
nationality evidence remains unknown.

Role Function still describes *what the work is*: direct research, research-adjacent engineering,
clinical implementation, technical platform, non-research, or unknown. Thus a BCI clinical role
can have strong domain relevance while remaining clinical implementation; an embedded role can be
transferable technical work; and a neural-decoding internship can be a near-term research path.
